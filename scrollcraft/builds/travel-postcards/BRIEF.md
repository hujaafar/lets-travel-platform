# Travel postcards

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Evidence and authored direction

1. Vibe: the user asks for "same style" but "not same images", "something for traveling" and for users to "feel comfortable". Authored direction: cinematic, warm, spacious, welcoming.
2. Journey: keep the working travel product. Authored sequence: coastal window opens, choose a travel interest, compare actual trips, explore a destination, find personal recommendations.
3. Energy: one short, expressive opening, then quiet search and booking surfaces. Remove the long alpine climb and moving slogan strip.
4. Feeling: invitation at entry; possibility as a postcard opens into a landscape; confidence at the searchable collection; curiosity at the destination spread; reassurance at the personal close.
5. Signature: a coastal travel photograph opens out of its frame while a foreground postcard moves independently, like opening a travel journal.
6. Aesthetic: editorial travel photography, warm ivory type on deep green, clear labels, comfortable type sizes. Preserve the product wordmark.
7. Structure: distinct scenes with native scrolling. The earlier reference's immersive scale and depth inform the choreography, not its snow terrain, assets or source.
8. Assets: inspected existing Uluwatu coastline, Bali temple and Greek island photographs. Use these authorized repository assets. No invented travelers, testimonials, stock availability or prices.

## Feeling curve and peak

Opening: welcome through stable headline and an immediately available journey action.
Expansion: anticipation through the coast growing out of its photographic frame.
Peak: "The postcard opened and I was looking out over the ocean." This happens within the opening's short scroll span.
Collection: confidence through readable dates, duration, price and searchable trips.
Destination spread: curiosity through real trip selection and direct itinerary actions.
Close: ease through smaller typography, clear account navigation and personal recommendations.

Tell-someone sentence: It's the site where a travel postcard opens into an ocean view, then helps you find a trip that feels like you.

## Layer contract and scroll score

| Plane | Independent movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Dark editorial ground | Stable | Readable text at all times |
| Uluwatu photographic window | Expands sideways and vertically with scroll | No extracted subjects or duplicated photographic geometry |
| Coastal image inside window | Gentle ambient pan and smaller scroll scale | Stops offscreen, when hidden or paused |
| Bali postcard in foreground | Small opposite translation and rotation | Overlaps photograph, never covers the headline or primary action |
| Route drawing | Draws from departure to destination during expansion | Decorative, no invented route distances |
| Copy and actions | Stable layout, small displacement only on large screens | Semantic HTML, always reachable |

Four device families: expanding photographic window; staggered collection reveals; destination wipe and traced route; lateral editorial close. No neighboring repeated devices. Quiet working surfaces are intentional, not dead pinned scroll.

Desktop opening: 175svh; mobile: natural-flow composition with local image depth instead of a long pinned screen. System reduced motion and explicit pause remove pinning and decorative motion. Direct category buttons filter the actual collection. Existing booking, payment, manager and admin behavior remains intact.

This is a product revision of the existing journal, not a claim that four fingerprint dimensions are new. No video generation or encoding is required; the previously recorded missing FFmpeg/KIE key does not affect the native photographic implementation.
