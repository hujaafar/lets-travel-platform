Vibe: photographic, editorial, kinetic. Existing cream/olive/terracotta identity and real itinerary photos remain.

Devices: hero parallax/scale; collection image-mask reveals; pinned scenic photo wipes with route drawing; lateral manifesto type; working-page and dialog entry movement. Native scroll, no wheel interception, no pointer capture. Mobile gets a shorter pinned stage and larger direct controls; reduced motion gets a static scene with functional buttons.
