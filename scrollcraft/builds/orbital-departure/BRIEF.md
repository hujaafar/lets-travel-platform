# Orbital departure

Self-authored under continuing creative delegation. Latest user direction: remove “Design preview · sample data”, “I DONT SEE SCROLL EFFECTS”, and use https://arstraumur.music/ as the visual reference.

1. Vibe: cosmic, expansive, tactile, cinematic. The supplied reference visibly travels past a photoreal Earth, Moon and Saturn as scrolling changes camera position and scale.
2. Sequence: orbital Earth → rotating route → photographic arrival → operational travel desk → itinerary and collection.
3. Energy: immediate visible camera motion, an expanding arrival aperture as the peak, then useful native-flow administration and existing gallery.
4. Feeling curve: wonder from the lit Earth; anticipation from the moving route; immersion when a destination fills the frame; agency from direct access to the actual saved plan and administration.
5. Peak / tell-someone sentence: “It's the travel workspace where you scroll down from orbit and land inside your next destination.”
6. More expressive than the prior editorial opening: ink-black space, warm ivory typography, vermilion route accents. Keep the custom TP mark and functional navigation.
7. Authored decision: one finite continuous camera opening followed by distinct existing desk scenes, not an entire application trapped in a flight. Keep native scrolling, keyboard navigation and a direct skip. Mobile gets the same opening motion with separate composition and shorter travel.
8. Assets: existing local destination photographs; NASA Blue Marble texture credited in docs/licenses; native WebGL sphere, CSS/SVG stars and route geometry. No copied reference code, branding, audio or models. No new dependencies or generated media.

## Layer contract and score

Background: two deterministic star planes translating at different depths. Subject: a textured, directionally lit sphere rotating with scroll. Foreground: route orbit and typography moving independently. Arrival: a circular aperture opens onto the actual featured plan photograph; the Earth recedes while image scale settles. Route geometry is decorative, not a claim about real flight tracking.

The opening starts in the first viewport and responds to the first wheel/touch movement at every width. There is no timed loader or silent intro. Chapter buttons are direct timeline controls; a motion control respects the OS by default and permits an explicit on/off preference. A static complete composition replaces pinning when motion is off; no user action is hidden by opacity alone.
